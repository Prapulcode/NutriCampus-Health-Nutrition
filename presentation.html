Context & Motivation: Student Health on Campus

⏰ The Undergraduate Reality
  • Rigorous Schedules: Long lecture hours, programming laboratories, and project submissions leave little time for meal planning.
  • Irregular Meal Patterns: Skipping breakfast, late-night dinners, and erratic snacking are widespread daily habits.
  • Hostel & Mess Monotony: Reliance on limited hostel menus and oily canteen snacks leads to nutritional deficits.
📉 The Health & Focus Gap
  • Afternoon Brain Fog: Heavy refined carbohydrate lunches cause sharp 2:00 PM lecture drowsiness and sluggishness.
  • Chronic Dehydration: Students forget to drink water, causing fatigue and tension headaches in coding labs.
  • Internet Fad Vulnerability: Social media promotes unverified, restrictive diets unsuitable for college students.
💡 Why NutriCampus?
  • Student-Friendly Awareness: Evidence-backed nutrition advice centered on accessible, budget-friendly everyday foods.
  • Interactive Tools: Simple BMI screening, fluid tracking with visual bottle animation, and canteen food swaps.
  • Non-Clinical & Safe: 100% educational guidance that avoids medical diagnosis or clinical replacement.

Problem Statement & Non-Diagnostic Boundary

🚨 Problem Statement

College students frequently encounter difficulty finding simple, reliable, and accessible information on balanced diets, daily hydration, and wellness routines.

  • Busy academic schedules and erratic meal routines trigger heavy reliance on instant noodles, canteen fried foods, and high-caffeine beverages.
  • Dehydration impairs cognitive stamina, attention span, and debugging focus in laboratory sessions.
  • Absence of a localized, student-friendly platform combining clear nutritional education with interactive awareness tools.
⚠️ Mandatory Non-Diagnostic Boundary

In strict alignment with the 25CPD37 course syllabus and ethical medical software standards:

  • Educational Awareness Only: The platform is built solely for educational lifestyle guidance; it does NOT diagnose health conditions.
  • No Clinical Prescriptions: Does not prescribe pharmacological supplements, meal therapies, or clinical cures.
  • Screening Limitation: Explains that BMI is a screening ratio, not a body fat diagnosis.
  • Referral Protocol: Explicitly encourages consulting campus medical officers or certified dietitians.

System Architecture & Technology Stack

💻 Technology Stack
  • Frontend Layer:
    • HTML5: Semantic markup, accessible tab navigation, form validation.
    • CSS3: Custom CSS variables, Flexbox/Grid, Dark/Light theme switching, fluid keyframe liquid animations.
    • JavaScript (ES6+): Client-side state machine, BMI calculations, dynamic meter pointer, interactive quiz logic.

    • Python 3 & Flask (app.py) / Standard Library (local_server.py).

  • Strict Privacy Preservation: Sensitive metrics (height, weight, daily water intake) remain strictly on the user's local device.
  • Mobile-First Responsiveness: Fluidly adapts from smartphone screens (360px) to computer lab displays (1920px).
  • Modular Decoupling: Nutrition Guide, BMI Calculator, Water Tracker, Quiz, and Feedback survey operate as independent modules.

MODULE 2 • NUTRITION EDUCATION

Module 2: Nutrition Guide & The 50-25-25 College Plate

🍽️ The 50-25-25 College Plate Model

A simple visual rule endorsed by Harvard Health & ICMR-NIN public health standards that eliminates tedious calorie counting:

  • 50% Plate: Colorful Vegetables & Fruits: Delivers essential dietary fiber, Vitamin C, and hydration; prevents mid-day sugar spikes.
  • 25% Plate: Complex Whole Grains: Whole wheat rotis, brown/parboiled rice, ragi, jowar, oats; supplies sustained glucose release for coding labs.
  • 25% Plate: Quality Protein & Pulses: Dal, rajma, chole, paneer, eggs, sprouts, and curd; fuels muscle recovery and lasting satiety.
  • Side Drink: Water or fresh buttermilk (chaas) rather than sugary sodas.
🥗 Food Group Explorer & Superfoods
  • Interactive Category Filter: Students can filter cards by Carbs, Proteins, Healthy Fats, Micronutrients, and Hydration & Fiber.
  • Budget-Friendly Student Superfoods:
    • Roasted Peanuts & Chana: Inexpensive, shelf-stable protein for study desks.
    • Curd & Plain Dahi: Probiotics supporting digestive comfort and focus.
    • Bananas & Seasonal Fruit: Natural quick-energy fuel for lectures.
    • Boiled Eggs / Paneer: Complete amino-acid sources.
  • Hostel Room Tip: Keeping roasted chana or peanuts at the desk helps students resist midnight junk food cravings.

MODULE 3 • INTERACTIVE SCREENING TOOL

Module 3: BMI Calculator & Screening Tool

⚖️ Calculation Engine & Visual Gauge
  • Dual-Unit Input Flexibility:
    • Metric Mode: Height in cm, Weight in kg.
    • Imperial Mode: Height in feet & inches, Weight in lbs.
  • Formula: BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²
  • Dynamic Visual Gauge Meter: Interactive pointer translates across 4 WHO standard classification zones:
    • Blue: Underweight (< 18.5)
    • Green: Normal / Healthy Range (18.5 – 24.9)
    • Amber: Overweight (25.0 – 29.9)
    • Red: Obesity Range (≥ 30.0)
  • Healthy Weight Guidance: Computes healthy weight bounds for the student's exact height.
⚠️ Prominent Medical Disclaimer

Prominently highlighted to fulfill institutional project guidelines and medical ethics:

  • Screening Ratio Only: BMI is an epidemiological ratio (kg/m²), NOT a clinical diagnosis of body fat percentage, fitness, or metabolic health.
  • Athletic Muscle Inaccuracy: BMI cannot differentiate between muscle mass and adipose tissue. Active athletes naturally record higher BMI while being in peak health.
  • Neutral Counseling: Displays supportive, non-stigmatizing lifestyle advice, discouraging crash diets.
  • Clinical Referral: Recommends consulting campus health clinic physicians for comprehensive evaluations.

MODULE 5 • CAMPUS WELLNESS GUIDE

Module 5: Healthy Campus Habits & Wellness Routines

💻 20-20-20 Screen Rule
Every 20 minutes, gaze at an object 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Crucial for engineering students to relieve corneal strain during 3-hour programming labs.
🧠 Exam Brain Fuel
Eat a light balanced meal 90 minutes before exams (curd, banana, boiled egg). Heavy oily food shunts blood away from neural recall to digestion.
🌙 Study Snacking
Swap sweet biscuits and oily instant noodles for roasted makhana, peanuts, or warm milk to avoid insomnia-inducing insulin spikes.
😴 Circadian Sleep
Dim laptop screens with blue-light filters after 9 PM. Consistent 7-8 hours sleep consolidates newly learned code algorithms into long-term memory.
🧘 Lab Desk Stretches
Gentle 3-minute cervical shoulder rolls and lumbar twists alleviate chronic 'tech-neck' strain caused by prolonged laptop posture.
🥗 Mindful Meals
Eat meals away from YouTube or debug terminals. Undistracted eating ensures proper satiety hormone signaling and prevents overeating.
