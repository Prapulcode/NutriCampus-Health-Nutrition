COMMUNITY PROJECT (25CPD37) • DEPT OF CSE, NHCE

NutriCampus: Health & Nutrition Web Application

🚨 Real-World Problem Overview
1-Sentence Problem Statement:
"Undergraduate students face irregular eating schedules, chronic lab dehydration, and high reliance on nutrient-poor canteen snacks with zero accessible guidance."
Core Pain Points:
  • Irregular meal hours during coding labs
  • Heavy canteen fried snacks and sodas
  • Afternoon brain fog and low daily hydration
  • Clinical nutrition tools are complex and intimidating
🎯 Core System Goal & Value
System Vision:
"Deliver a lightweight, zero-barrier responsive web application translating complex nutrition into realistic food swaps, hydration tracking, and screening."
Key Solution Highlights:
  • 100% Zero-install single page web architecture
  • Intuitive 50-25-25 balanced visual plate guide
  • Safe hydration tracking capped at 5.0 Liters
  • Validated non-diagnostic medical screening boundary

Slide 2: Scope & Target Users

📌 Project Scope Boundaries
In Scope (Version 1.0 Release):
  • 50-25-25 visual balanced meal guide
  • Practical Indian canteen food swaps
  • Anthropometric BMI screening calculator
  • Fluid water tracker with 5.0 L safety limit
  • 7-question gamified myth-busting quiz
  • Usability survey & community evaluation portal
Out of Scope (Exclusions):
  • No clinical medical diagnosis or prescriptions
  • No calorie counting or strict diet regimes
  • No external hardware or wearable sensors
👥 Target Users & Key Roles
Primary Student Users:
  • Hostel & Commuter Students: Budget food swaps and meal portioning.
  • Engineering Lab Students: Screen fatigue relief (20-20-20 rule) and hydration.
Evaluation & Mentorship Roles:
  • Peer Reviewers: 12+ CSE students testing usability and logging reviews.
  • Faculty Evaluators: Reviewing Design Thinking steps and requirements compliance.
Operating Environment:

Any mobile smartphone (Android/iOS) or PC browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari).

Slide 3: Functional Requirements & Architecture

🏗️ Use-Case & System Architecture
1. User Interaction (Student / Peer Reviewer) Responsive Web UI: Navigation, Calculators, Tracker, Quiz
2. Client-Side Controller (Vanilla JavaScript) Quetelet BMI Engine • 5L Fluid Clamping • Instant Quiz Logic
3. Storage & Dual-Tier Persistence • Browser localStorage: Offline hydration logs & state
• Python/Flask REST API: Asynchronous feedback sync
• SQLite Database (health_nutrition.db): Pilot evaluations

✓ Works 100% offline in any browser; seamlessly syncs when connected.

⚡ Top 5 Core Functional Requirements
  • FR-1: 50-25-25 Balanced Plate Visualizer: Interactive portions (50% veggies/fiber, 25% complex carbs, 25% lean protein).
  • FR-2: Campus Canteen & Food Swaps: Evidence-based swaps for instant noodles, sugary sodas, and fried snacks.
  • FR-3: Anthropometric BMI Screening: Metric/Imperial calculation with color gauge & mandatory medical disclaimer.
  • FR-4: Hydration Tracker with 5.0 L Limit: Animated fluid bottle with strict 5000 mL daily safety cap to prevent hyponatremia.
  • FR-5: Quiz & Community Feedback Portal: Gamified myth-busting badges and rating-free peer usability evaluation.

SYSTEM QUALITY & ETHICAL BOUNDARIES • COURSE 25CPD37

Slide 4: Non-Functional Requirements & Constraints

🛡️ Quality Standards & Security
Performance Standards:
  • Page load under 1.5 seconds on 4G
  • Lightweight bundle under 150 KB total
  • Instant client calculations under 16 ms
  • Zero external CSS/JS framework bloat
Security & Privacy Standards:
  • Zero mandatory personal information collection
  • No passwords or user accounts required
  • Parameterized SQL statements prevent injection
  • Strict HTML sanitization blocks XSS attacks
⚙️ Constraints & Ethical Boundary
Technical Constraints:
  • Cross-browser execution (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox)
  • Responsive viewports: 360px mobile to 4K desktop
  • Dual deployment: zero-dependency local server & Vercel edge
Mandatory Medical Ethics Boundary:
  • Strictly educational health awareness platform
  • BMI defined as screening ratio, not diagnostic tool
  • Prominent amber disclaimers visible on all screening views
  • Water tracker enforces 5.0 L daily safety limit
